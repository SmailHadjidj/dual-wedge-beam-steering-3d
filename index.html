Dual Wedge Beam Steering — 3D

Interactive visualization of two rotating optical wedges and the resulting laser scan.
Mouse: left-drag to rotate • wheel to zoom • right-drag to pan.

Educational model: beam deflection is represented as the vector sum of the two rotating wedges. Real optical deflection also depends on wedge angle, refractive index and detailed ray geometry.
